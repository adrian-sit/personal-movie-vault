## Notes
This is my first ever Wes Anderson movie I have ever watched, I have watched trailers The Grand Budapest Hotel, Asteroid City, and I'm pretty intrigued by such a unique style, therefore I was pretty excited to watch this. An amusing part to me is seeing a lot of A-list actors playing small roles, like Tom Hanks, Scarlett Johansson, Benedict Cumberbatch and Bryan Cranston from my favorite TV show of all time Breaking Bad. Experiencing a very uncommon style from mainstream movies is a great experience, however I didn't think there were enough good jokes for a comedy movie, beyond some that were shown in the trailer. However, I think I would like to watch Wes Anderson movies again.
