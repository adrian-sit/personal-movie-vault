## Notes
I haven't watched marvel movies for a long time when I watched this movie, therefore I wasn't caught up with the background story. I remember seeing a few teenagers walked out in disbelief after seeing Thunderbolts marketed as "The New Avengers" in the end of the movie. The most memorable part is I watched this movie in Screen X, which is a format where the screen is extended to the left and right side of the walls in the theatre. I think this format is pretty cool, it does add to the immersion a little bit for certain fight scenes, but not necessary for this movie. For the movie itself, I think it is a standard decent Marvel movie, decent comedic scenes with decent action scenes, but nothing too memorable. The idea of Bob and the Void is pretty interesting though.
