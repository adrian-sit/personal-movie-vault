## Notes
Due to traffic, I arrived at the movie 30 minutes late, I got in when Lightning McQueen is in court, but overall I am able to follow the movie, and I also catched up with the plot online afterwards. I had a mildly high expectation for this movie because I remember watching Toy Story which is another more than 2 decade old movie, the animation quality are noticeably worse compared to animation movies you see nowadays, but I was genuinely attached to the story. However in this case of Cars, even though there is clear plot development, conflict, character development, I don't think they are done well enough to develop strong feelings for any of the scenes. 

For example, the idea of the Hudson Hornet having a career ending disastrous crash inspired Ligtning to give up the Pison Cup victory to help the King was a cool scene, understanding he used to be very self-centered but changed, but it doesn't feel powerful. Lightning's decision to establish his racing headquaters in Radiator Springs revitalizing the town is also cool, especially because this is Sally's dream, but there aren't enough interactions between Lightning and everyone in Radiator Springs to feel why Lightning would care about them so much, other than the single deeper conversation Lightning had with Sally.
