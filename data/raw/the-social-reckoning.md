## Notes
The main reason I am watching this movie is from the trailer I can see there are multiple scenes shot in the SFU Burnaby campus, I wanted to know how they are going to use the location for the scenes. There aren't many surprises, most of the SFU campus scenes are already shown in the trailer. 

Overall the movie is good. I enjoy this type of drama movies, though the plot is fairly straightforward without too many surprises. I know this is based on a real story, and I liked how it didn't over invent other elements to add to the movie. However, I regret buying the premium format AVX for this movie, there are no scenes in this movie that would benefit from a clearer high definition screen or better sound, with most of the movie focusing on pure dialogue for story progression.
