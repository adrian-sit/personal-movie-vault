## Notes
Growing up playing Minecraft, I was pretty excited to see the game finally made into a movie, even though I haven't played the game for years. This is the movie that started my phase of watching movies in movie theatres. I watched this in a pretty quiet VIP section, therefore there were no kids yelling or throwing popcorn when the "chicken jockey" scene came on. The most memorable part of the experience is trying tacos for the first time in my life, I had the fish and chicken taco, both are pretty good.
