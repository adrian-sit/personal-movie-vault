## Notes
One of the most beautiful movies I have ever watched. The point of watching movies in movie theatres is to feel moved by the scenes, and I think this movie does it so beautifully with all elements including the music, story, dance moves, and characters. After watching the movie, I found myself putting a lot of songs on repeat in my playlist, as well as rewatching my favorite scenes a lovely night and epilogue, where Sebastian and Mia imagined a life they would have had together.
