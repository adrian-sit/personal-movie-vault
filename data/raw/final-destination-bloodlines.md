## Notes
One of my favorite movies of the year. One part I really like is how unpredictable the characeters' demise are, walking into this movie you know there will be some brutal scenes, but they are almost always not exactly how I predicted it. They make you think the character will pass this way, but then end up surviving, and then somehow passing in another brutal way. This element of surprise really adds to the experience. The movie gave decent screentime to know about the characters which makes you root for them and want them to live, and the rational father who believes Iris is a crazy woman also makes the whole movie feels realistic.

The movie also had a comical and fitting playlist, including "Fallin" performed before the fall of the skyview tower, "Raindrops keep falling on my head" during the falling of the skyview tower, and "Without You" performed by Air Supply from Erik's scene at the tattoo parlour, which became one of the most played songs in my playlist in 2025. The way they cut the scenes, while accompanied by the music, is the main reason I rated this movie so high. 

Another thing to note, I really liked William John Bludworth (JB)'s scene, I like how they made him the kid from the skyview tower, tying his mysterious identity throughout the Final Destination franchise, and his line "Life is precious, enjoy every second, you never know when".
