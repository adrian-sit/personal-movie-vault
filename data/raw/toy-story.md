## Notes
I didn't have high expectations coming into this movie, since I have seen many high budget animation movies with astonishing quality, and Toy Story is a 3 decade old movie. However, I remember leaving the theatre feeling extremely impressed. The character interactions between Woody and Buzz are amazing to watch, from Woody's jealousy, Buzz's existential crisis, and the way they are able to resolve their differences through the new experiences they have is really well done. The whole story is really fun to watch, and it defenitely does not feel like a movie that is only made for kids. 

The idea of toys having life and prentending to be lifeless when kids are playing with them, is a really cool concept. They way the wrote a scene where they intentionally came to life in front of Sid is really satisfying.
