## Notes
Overall I enjoyed the movie. Seeing so many A-list actors in the main cast takes away from the immersion of the story a little bit, it is hard to not see Tom Holland and Zendaya as Spiderman and MJ, however it is understandably typical for a Christopher Nolan movie. Other than the story of the trojan horse, I haven't heard of the other Greek mythologies or stories mentioned in the movie, I have never read the book version of "The Odyssey" or other movie versions, making the whole viewing a very fresh experience. I really like Christopher Nolan's minimal use of CGI and preference of using real props, the movie has a lot of great visuals that made this movie immersive. 

I watched this movie in Cineplex Langley in IMAX 70MM, I bought the tickets a month in advance, getting a seat right in the middle, which adds to the experience.
