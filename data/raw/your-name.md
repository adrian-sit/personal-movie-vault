## Notes
I was pretty excited to watch this one knowing a lot of the animated locations are based on real life, and those locations became really famous after the movie came out. Even though the story is not a completely new concept, a guy and girl switching bodies, the idea of the switching takes place with the guy in the future, and the girl from the past, is such a cool concept. The movie also has pretty good comedic elements that keeps me entertained, and really good music pieces that sets the feelings for all the big moments.
