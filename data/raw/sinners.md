## Notes
On my first watch, I found the first half of the movie to be tough to follow, since it is difficult to understand every word of the African accent. However after the vampires started showing up to the juke joint, every scene that comes after it gets very intense. After watching movie reviews and reading about the plot online, I've learned the movie has themes tied to Africans' suppression and freedom. I rewatched the movie when they reshowed the IMAX version near Halloween. With how much emphasis this movie has on music, the IMAX version hits a lot stronger, there are many scenes that made me deeply immersed. I was able to learn more about the characters, which help me care about the characters.

From time to time, I think about Sammie's choice to disregard his father's pleas to seek salvation to continue pursuing music, even after the violent night involving the vampires. To Sammie, he thinks it was the greatest day of his life until the sun went down, and Stack also agrees as that was the only time they were truly free.
