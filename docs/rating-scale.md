# Personal Rating Scale

Ratings are personal scores on a 0–10 scale. They capture the viewing
experience rather than an objective assessment of a film's quality.

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Below 5 | Horribly made movie |
| 5–5.5 | Boring, physically cringe-worthy, or a waste of time and money, but not poorly made |
| 6–6.5 | Includes elements I enjoy, but is not very enjoyable overall |
| 7–7.5 | A movie I enjoyed, but would not necessarily recommend |
| 8–8.5 | Holds my attention, feels immersive or emotionally resonant, or provides an escape from reality |
| 9–10 | Exceptional: completely captivating, deeply emotional, and unforgettable |
